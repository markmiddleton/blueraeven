Jams & Spreads

Old-World Recipes, Farm-Fresh Flavor

We’ve been stirring, simmering, and perfecting jams and fruit spreads for years and in 2026 brought something we’ve dreamed about for a long time. We were finally certified to share our old-world family recipes with our wholesale partners, which means our jams and fruit spreads can now be found beyond our farmstands and in the stores you love.

Keep an eye out for these jars on your local shelves. Each one is made the way our family has always done; slowly, carefully, and with a time-honored method that brings out the true character of every berry. We’re thrilled to finally bring this tradition from our kitchen to yours, and we can’t wait for you to taste the difference an old-world recipe makes.
