Buying Wholesale

Artisanal quality and care, ready for your shelves.

Our mission is to provide farm-fresh, homemade worthy pies with the flakiest crust for all. We deliver fresh and frozen pies throughout the Northwest with the same artisanal quality and care as we serve at our own farmstand.

Pies made with our own fruit or sourced locally from producers that we know and trust. We bake in our full, family-sized 9″ pie tins with 5″ diameter mini pies for a delightful two-person treat.

Our Blue Raeven pies are delivered in a timeless blue and Kraft brown cardboard box, with the pie nestled on blue and white checkered paper for a farm-fresh, homemade appeal. Each box feels like it came from an artisan bakery.

An oval cutout window on the box top allows consumers to preview the beautiful handmade pie inside, each with our signature pie dough lettering on top signifying the variety of pie within. B is for blueberries; A is for apples; and so on.

We take pride in consistently producing quality that our customers have come to rely on. The Blue Raeven product line is labeled and packaged by hand, allowing us to inspect each individual pie prior to delivery.

Let’s Connect

Fundraising

Blue Raeven Pies Make Fundraising Delicious.

Across Oregon, communities connect over the simplest joys, and few things bring people together quite like a fresh, handcrafted pie. That’s why Blue Raeven partners with schools, teams, clubs, and organizations of every kind to make fundraising as easy as pie.

Our pies start with berries and fruit grown by local farmers who pour their heart into every harvest, and they end up on tables where families gather, celebrate, and support the causes they care about most.

When your organization chooses Blue Raeven, you’re not just raising funds—you’re sharing something made with real fruit, real craft, and real Oregon love created and produced by a local family business.

Because around here, we believe one thing wholeheartedly: pie fixes everything.
