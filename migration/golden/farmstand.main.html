Find Us on 99W

We’re located on the scenic Highway 99W corridor, in the heart of Oregon wine country. Whether you’re visiting the Willamette Valley wineries or making a special trip just for pie, we’re easy to find.

Look for our blue farmstand sign on the west side of the highway, just south of Amity. Plenty of free parking available.
